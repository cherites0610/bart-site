# Surface: Home and site shell (index)

Mode: Experience. The work leads from the first viewport.

Audience and job: recruiters, interviewers and prospective collaborators who want to see what Bart (廖柏安) has built and how to reach him. Content: no real projects or posts exist yet; use clearly labeled placeholders. Constraints: Astro static site, zh-Hant only, light JS, dark/light support.

Chosen direction: 集郵冊 (stamp album). Memorable moment: the latest project is a large stamp on an album page with today's postmark.

Decided: positioning line "我是全端開發者，從人的需求出發，把事情做成系統。" sits next to the name, one size step below it. Projects have individual detail pages (the stamp enlarged, with its description). Fonts: Noto Serif TC bold for the name and headings, Noto Sans TC for body and articles, JetBrains Mono for postmarks, dates, numbers and code; Google Fonts sliced loading for the Chinese faces; body line height about 1.8, about 35 full-width characters per line.

Page mapping: Home is the cover page; Portfolio is the album pages of project stamps; Blog is a list of postmarked envelopes ordered by date; About is a single page.

Unresolved: dark/light palette values, exact stamp sizes and placement rules, motion details.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a stamp album. Each project is a mounted stamp, each blog post a postmarked envelope; it refuses the grid of equal cards under a hero.

OWN-WORLD: Deep navy album pages, off-white perforated stamps in varied sizes, post-office green for structure, postmark red for dates and the active state. Round date cancellation marks, hinge strips, glassine edges. Recognizable with all content removed.

STORY: The visitor sees what Bart has built, understands the kind of developer he is from the stamps, and clicks one stamp to read the project or writes to him. Unfinished work is a visible "待蓋郵戳" slot, never a blank gap.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Navy album page filling the viewport. The name 廖柏安 Bart is set large as the page title top left with the positioning line beneath it; one large stamp (latest project, placeholder) holds the main position, a block of smaller stamps sits beside it, and a round postmark with today's date overlaps the top right corner. The contact link sits as a small stamp-shaped action bottom left.

FORM: stamp album, grounded candidate 5 of 7 in the second re-roll hand; seed: seed:39a2625a75ce2a18.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
